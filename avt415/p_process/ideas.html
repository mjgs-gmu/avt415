P1.1 : Ideas

The Phoenix Press:

Bookstore that sells zines, stickers, prints and other accessories from aspiring writers and artists.

Kool Club:

A shop that offers monthly themes box service with mystery items such as stickers, postcards, mini prints, greeting cards and bookmarks.

Patchwork Drip:

A shop that offers aparrel and accessories such as t-shirts, tote bags, hoodies, caps, and more. Artists creates and upload their designs for the customers to purchase, and the shop manages the printing and shipping of the items.
