User Scenario Mapping

By Mj Gomez-Salazar

Scenario:
Erin is a college student majoring in Zoology that lives in the college dorms. Erin is sad that other students including her roommate receive packages from their families and friends. Additionally, she feels disconnected from the typical online shopping experience as it lacks personal touch. While searching for mail clubs, she came across a website called “Critter Club” that offers monthly subscription of animals themed collectibles. Erin decides to subscribe for the mystery theme box that is filled with stickers, postcards, prints, and bookmarks because it builds excitement and is connected to their passion for animals.
Actor:
Erin
Motivator:
She feels disconnected because they rarely receive packages while their roommate frequently does. Online shopping doesn;t fulfill her need because it feels something else rather than something meaningful.
Intention/Intent:
She explores various mail clubs that align her interests and have an emotional connection to it.
Action:
She views the subscriptions from “Critter Club” and scroll through their products.
Resolution:
Erin subscribes for the mystery package and looks forward each month for their critter themed package.
